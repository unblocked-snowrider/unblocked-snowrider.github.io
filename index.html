How to Get a High Score in Snow Rider 3D

1. Keep Your Eyes on the Horizon

Do not stare at your sled—look at the top third of the slope. Spotting rolling snowballs and narrow double-tree gates half a second earlier gives you time to line up cleanly without panic-steering.

2. Stay Near the Center Lane

Riding near the left or right edge of the mountain traps you whenever a snowman or boulder spawns ahead. After every dodge, drift back toward the middle so you always have two escape lanes.
