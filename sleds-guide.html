1. How Gift Boxes & Sled Unlocks Work

Every red-and-gold gift box you collect on the slope is added permanently to your browser's local storage balance. Even when you crash, you keep every present gathered during that run. Open the Sled Shop on the main menu to spend your gifts on custom sleighs—from wooden toboggans and racing bobsleds to Santa's sleigh and futuristic winter rides.

Fastest Way to Farm Gifts

  • Prioritize survival over risky grabs: Staying alive past score 40 spawns far more gift clusters per minute than crashing repeatedly at score 10.
  • Line up before landing ramps: Straight rows of 2–3 gifts frequently appear right after wooden ramps—center your sled mid-air to sweep them cleanly.
  • Skip corner traps: Never swerve into a tight gap between a snowman and a pine tree for a single present.

2. Five Pro Habits for Breaking 100+ Score

  1. Center-Lane Discipline: Always return toward the middle of the slope after dodging an obstacle so you have room to move either left or right.
  2. Micro-Tap Steering: Use rapid short taps on A/D or ←/→ instead of holding keys down.
  3. Read Snowball Angles Early: Rolling snowballs cross the hill diagonally. Steer into the space they just passed rather than racing ahead of them.
  4. Straight Ramp Entries: Approach wooden ramps straight on—hitting a ramp sideways launches you directly into trees on the landing slope.
  5. Always Play Fullscreen: Expanding the canvas lets you spot distant hazards on the horizon earlier when downhill speed ramps up.
